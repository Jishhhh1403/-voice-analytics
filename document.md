# Customer Problem Prioritisation Measures

## 1. Customer Impact Measures

1. **Problem Volume** – Total number of feedback records reporting the problem.
2. **Unique Customers Affected** – Number of distinct customers reporting the problem.
3. **Customer Churn Risk** – Likelihood that the problem contributes to customer attrition.
4. **Customer Retention Impact** – Potential negative effect on customer retention.
5. **Customer Frustration Level** – Intensity of frustration expressed in feedback.
6. **Customer Effort Score** – Effort required to complete the affected banking journey.
7. **Task Completion Failure** – Extent to which customers cannot complete their intended task.
8. **Journey Abandonment Rate** – Percentage of reported journeys that customers abandon, where measurable.
9. **Repeat Complaint Frequency** – How frequently customers report the same problem repeatedly.
10. **Customer Trust Impact** – Potential damage to customer confidence in the bank.
11. **Customer Segment Impact** – Whether the problem disproportionately affects important customer segments.
12. **Customer Lifetime Value at Risk** – Estimated customer value exposed to the problem.

## 2. Risk and Compliance Measures

13. **Regulatory Risk** – Potential violation of banking regulations or regulatory requirements.
14. **Financial Security Risk** – Potential exposure involving customer funds or financial transactions.
15. **Data Privacy Risk** – Potential exposure of sensitive customer information.
16. **Cybersecurity Risk** – Potential security vulnerabilities or unauthorised access.
17. **Fraud Exposure** – Potential for fraudulent transactions or misuse.
18. **Transaction Integrity Risk** – Risk of incorrect, duplicated or incomplete transactions.
19. **Consumer Protection Risk** – Potential violation of customer rights or fair-treatment obligations.
20. **Legal and Litigation Risk** – Potential legal consequences arising from the problem.
21. **Audit and Compliance Exposure** – Potential adverse audit findings or compliance failures.
22. **Reputational Risk** – Potential damage to the bank's public reputation.

## 3. Operational Impact Measures

23. **Operational Cost** – Cost incurred by the bank due to the problem.
24. **Customer Support Volume** – Number of support interactions caused by the problem.
25. **Issue Resolution Time** – Time required to resolve the reported problem.
26. **Manual Intervention Required** – Extent to which employees must intervene to resolve the issue.
27. **Back-office Workload** – Additional operational workload generated.
28. **Incident Frequency** – Frequency of recurring operational incidents.
29. **Service Availability Impact** – Extent to which customers cannot access a banking service.
30. **Cross-functional Dependency** – Number of teams or systems involved in resolving the issue.
31. **Operational Disruption** – Degree to which the problem disrupts normal banking operations.

## 4. Financial and Business Impact Measures

32. **Revenue Loss** – Potential revenue lost due to the customer problem.
33. **Transaction Value at Risk** – Monetary value of affected transactions.
34. **Transaction Failure Cost** – Financial cost associated with failed transactions.
35. **Refund and Compensation Cost** – Cost of refunds, reimbursements and customer compensation.
36. **Customer Acquisition Impact** – Potential effect on acquiring new customers.
37. **Cross-selling Opportunity Loss** – Potential loss of opportunities to sell additional banking products.
38. **Customer Lifetime Value Impact** – Potential reduction in long-term customer value.
39. **Cost-to-Serve Impact** – Increase in the cost of serving affected customers.

## 5. Problem Criticality and Technical Impact

40. **Problem Severity** – Overall seriousness of the customer problem.
41. **Business Criticality** – Importance of the affected banking functionality to core business operations.
42. **Functional Blockage** – Whether the issue completely prevents a customer from completing a task.
43. **Financial Transaction Criticality** – Whether the problem affects essential financial transactions.
44. **Problem Recurrence** – How frequently the same issue reappears.
45. **System-Wide Impact** – Whether the problem affects one feature or multiple banking journeys.
46. **Problem Dependency Impact** – Whether the issue creates downstream problems in other features.
47. **Failure Scope** – Whether the issue affects a specific platform, app version or a broader customer population.

## 6. Trend and Emerging Risk Measures

48. **Problem Growth Rate** – Rate at which reported occurrences are increasing.
49. **Emerging Problem Score** – Degree to which a previously uncommon problem is becoming prominent.
50. **Problem Acceleration** – Whether the rate of problem growth is itself increasing.
51. **Seasonality** – Whether the problem occurs more frequently during particular periods.
52. **Post-release Problem Spike** – Increase in complaints following an application release.
53. **Problem Persistence** – How long a problem continues to appear in customer feedback.
54. **Resolution Trend** – Whether reported problems are declining after interventions.
55. **Unresolved Problem Backlog** – Number of identified problems that remain unaddressed.

## 7. Strategic and Product Alignment Measures

56. **Strategic Goal Alignment** – Alignment with the bank's current strategic objectives.
57. **Product Roadmap Alignment** – Relevance to the existing product roadmap.
58. **Digital Adoption Impact** – Potential impact on adoption of digital banking services.
59. **Customer Experience Differentiation** – Whether resolving the issue improves competitive positioning.
60. **Feature Adoption Impact** – Effect on adoption of the affected banking feature.
61. **Innovation Opportunity** – Potential to create a meaningful improvement in the customer experience.
62. **Business Initiative Alignment** – Alignment with current organisational initiatives.
63. **Market Competitiveness** – Whether the problem places the bank at a disadvantage compared with competing banking applications.

## 8. Evidence Quality and Confidence Measures

64. **Evidence Strength** – Quality and reliability of supporting customer feedback.
65. **Source Diversity** – Number of independent feedback channels reporting the problem.
66. **Sentiment Consistency** – Consistency of negative sentiment across supporting feedback.
67. **Extraction Confidence** – Confidence in the NLP model's identification of the problem.
68. **Problem Classification Confidence** – Confidence in assigning feedback to a canonical problem.
69. **Journey Mapping Confidence** – Confidence in mapping the problem to a banking journey.
70. **Evidence Recency** – How recently customers have reported the problem.
71. **Human Validation Status** – Whether the problem has been reviewed and confirmed by a domain expert.
